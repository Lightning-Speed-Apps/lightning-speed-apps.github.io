How it works

Four modes.

Pick the reading style that fits your brain.

RSVP Mode

Words flash one at a time right in front of you, so your eyes never wander. Set your WPM, hit play, and let the words come to you. Adjust speed on the fly, skip ahead, or rewind. Works best when you want to push your pace.

Highlight Mode

The full article or chapter stays on screen while each word is highlighted and auto-scrolled as you read. It is easier to follow for longer sessions because you keep context without losing speed. Great for dense material where re-reading a sentence matters.

Line Highlight Mode

Read line by line with the current sentence brought into focus as the page moves with you. It gives you more context than single-word RSVP while still keeping your eyes anchored, which is especially useful for dialogue, long chapters, and slower material.

Classic E-reader

Sometimes you just want to read. The built-in e-reader gives you a clean, distraction-free view with customizable fonts, font sizes, and line spacing. Chapter navigation, notes, and progress tracking included.

When you import an article, Substack post, or AO3 fanfiction by URL, the app fetches the content directly from that third-party website on your behalf. The fetched text is parsed and stored locally on your device. We do not receive, log, or store the URLs you import or the content retrieved.
